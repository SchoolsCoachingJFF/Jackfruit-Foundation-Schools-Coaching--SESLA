# School Coaching Visit Survey

## Instructions

Use this form during the school visit. Complete the sections in order and record only what you have seen in records or observed during the visit.

- Do not write children's names.
- If there is a suspected abuse or danger, escalate immediately to the programme lead.
- Rate the evidence, not the director.
- Use the following rating scale: Established, In progress, Needs support, Urgent.
